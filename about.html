About Our Academy

Founded in 2015, Grandmaster Chess Academy was established with the vision of nurturing young chess talent and promoting strategic thinking among students of all ages. Over the years, we have trained hundreds of students, helping them build strong chess foundations and achieve success in competitive tournaments.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality chess education that develops critical thinking, problem-solving abilities, patience, and confidence while preparing students for competitive success.

Our Vision

To become one of the leading chess academies by creating future champions and inspiring lifelong learning through the game of chess.
